### 6.6 Final Program of "Optimal Integer Multiplication"

Initialization of algorithm contains more variables
than it shown in images:

1. AND R0, R0, #0 | Sign
1. AND R3, R3, #0 | Result
1. AND R4, R4, #0 | Sum Counter
1. AND R5, R5, #0 | Addend (initialized to highlight a variable)
1. ADD R1, R1, #0 | Generate Condition R1 - 0
1. BRn X
1. BRnzp Y
1. X -> ADD R0, R0, #1
1. NOT R1, R1
1. ADD R1, R1, #1
1. Y -> ADD R2, R2, #0 | Generate Condition R2 - 0
1. BRn X2
1. BRnzp Y2
1. X2 -> ADD R0, R0, #-1
1. NOT R2, R2
1. ADD R2, R2, #1
1. Y2 -> NOT R5, R2 | Use Addend as Temp condition var
1. ADD R5, R5, #1
1. ADD R5, R1, R5 | Generate Condition R1 - R2
1. BRn X3 | R1 < R2 true
1. ADD R5, R1, #0
1. ADD R4, R2, #0
1. BRnzp Y3
1. X3 -> ADD R5, R2, #0
1. ADD R4, R1, #0
1. Y3, W -> ADD R4, R4, #-1 | Generate Condition Counter(R4) - 1
1. BRn Z | Counter(R4) <= 0
1. ADD R3, R3, R5
1. BRnzp W
1. Z -> ADD R0, R0, #0 | Generate Condition Sign(R0) - 1
1. BRz X4 | R0 = 0 ?
1. NOT R3, R3
1. ADD R3, R3, #1
1. BRnzp X4
1. X4 -> TRAP x25 | HALT
