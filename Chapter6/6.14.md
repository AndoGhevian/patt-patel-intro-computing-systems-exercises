What are possible initial value of R1 given final R2 is 3.

#### Answer

if R2 is 3 then (R1 - 3) expression 3 times of condition generation gives non-negative value, and then on 4th condition test one of R1 - 1 operations give's negative.

So R1 >= 9 but R1 < 12, so R1 is from range [9;12)
