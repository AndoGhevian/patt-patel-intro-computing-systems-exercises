1. SKIP -> x8009
1. A -> x8010, LC is added by 6 for 6 sequential locations + 1
  for next instruction after block of words of length 6.
1. B -> x8017
1. BANNER -> x8018
1. C -> x8031

then we translate instructions:
1. line 5: (addr x8006) LD R1, A          -> 0010 001  000000011
