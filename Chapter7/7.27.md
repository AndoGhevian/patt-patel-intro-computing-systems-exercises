### 7.27

There are 2 register arguments:
R0 contains value to shift right by R1.

R1 should be positive value, otherwise
it defaults to 1.

R0 can contain any bit representation.

R6 contains the result of right shift: result is zero-extened.

example:
1. x0831 = 0000 1000 0011 0001
1. shifted: 0000 0100 0001 1000 = x0418

tested on simulator
