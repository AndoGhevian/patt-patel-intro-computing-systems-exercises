### 5.31

There are several cases, consider fact that registers all are positive:

1. register added to immediate representation of negative
number
1. 2 registers added and resul is overflow
1. register and immediate added and result is overflow

#### register + negative imm:

x1000 0001 101 000 1 11000

there is no other case, because 5 bits can represent minimum
of -16 and minimum register content is x1111.

#### register + register overflow

Maximum overflowed result we can get when adding
2 maximum contents: R7.

x7777 + x7777 = xEEEE, so there is no case to reach
xFFF8

#### register + positive imm overflow

this is also not the case:

x7777 + x000F = x7786
