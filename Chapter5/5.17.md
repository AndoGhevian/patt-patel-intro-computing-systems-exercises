### 5.17 How many times does the LC-3 make a read or write request to memory during the processing of the LD instruction? How many times during the processing of the LDI instruction? How many times during the processing of the LEA instruction? Processing includes all phases of the instruction cycle.

For LD it put evaluated address into MAR and reads it to MDR.
Including fetch of instruction it will be 2 times.

LDI read contents of MAR into MDR (1 read), then put that
contents to MAR and again read memory (2 read) into MDR.

2 memaccess + 1 fetch = 3 times

LEA does not access memory at all, it simply evaluate address.
fetch = 1 time.
