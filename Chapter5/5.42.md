### 5.42

Lets compare which one of those requires more instruction cycles
to perform to accomplish the task if it is emulated, and then choose the one that needs maximum number of those.

a. MOVE Ri, Rj; The contents of Rj are copied into Ri.

1. ADD Ri, Rj, #0

b. NAND Ri, Rj, Rk; Ri is the bit-wise NAND of Rj, Rk

1. AND Ri, Rj, Rk
1. NOT Ri, Ri

c. SHFL Ri, Rj, #2; The contents of Rj are shifted left 2 bits and stored into Ri.

For different immediate value support wee need to implement a loop.

1. x3000 AND Rx, Rx, #0
1. x3001 AND Ri, Ri, #0
1. x3002 ADD Ri, Rj, #0 Ri <- Rj
1. x3003 ADD Rx, Rx, #2 (init with any imm value > 0)
1. x3004 BRz x3008
1. x3005 ADD Ri, Ri, Ri
1. x3006 ADD Rx, Rx, #-1
1. x3007 BRnzp x3004
1. x3008

d. MUL Ri, Rj, Rk; Ri is the product of 2’s complement integers
in Rj, Rk.

1. x3000 AND Ri, Ri, #0
1. x3001 ADD Rk, Rk, #0
1. x3002 BRzp x3009
1. x3003 NOT Rj, Rj
1. x3004 ADD Rj, Rj, #1
1. x3005 NOT Rk, Rk
1. x3006 ADD Rk, Rk, #1 definitely non-negative integer
1. x3007 BRz x300B
1. x3008 ADD Ri, Rj, #0
1. x3009 ADD Rk, Rk, #-1
1. x300A BRnzp x3007
1. x300B

MUL requires 12 instruction cycles, maximum one,
and it can be used to omptimally implement SHFL simply
by multiplying numbers by powers of 2.
